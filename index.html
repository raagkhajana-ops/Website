About & Mission

A khajana — a treasury — of oral tradition

Gurmat sangeet has always been passed down orally, teacher to student. That intimacy is precious — and precarious. Raag Khajana digitally preserves recordings, notation, and oral history, and uses new tools, including machine learning, to make this knowledge more accessible.

We don't see technology as a replacement for the teacher-student relationship. We see it as a way to create more space for teachers to exist, and for more people to find their way to them.
